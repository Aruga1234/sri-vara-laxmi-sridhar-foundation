ABOUT US

Building opportunities for a stronger society.

Sri Vara Laxmi Sridhar Foundation is a Section 8 non-profit organisation committed to social and community development through education, skill development, technology, healthcare support, women empowerment, child welfare, entrepreneurship and humanitarian initiatives.

The Foundation seeks to empower people with knowledge, skills, resources and opportunities so that they can pursue education, employment, entrepreneurship and a more secure future.

FLAGSHIP PROJECT

HospitalizationUI — HealthTech Project

A proposed technology initiative intended to support the development of modern digital solutions for hospitalization and healthcare workflows.

HospitalizationUI is planned as an immediate project of the Foundation after securing suitable grant or incubator support. The project will bring together technology and healthcare-domain knowledge to develop practical HealthTech solutions.
